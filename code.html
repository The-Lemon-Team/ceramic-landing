Meet the Artisan

Elena Vance

Elena’s journey with clay began in a small village in northern Italy, where she spent summers watching local potters transform the earth into functional art. Today, she brings that same sense of wonder to her downtown studio.

Her process is a dialogue between the material and the maker. She believes that ceramics should not only be beautiful to look at but also a joy to hold—weighty enough to feel permanent, yet delicate enough to feel alive. Every piece is thrown by hand and glazed with custom-mixed minerals that react uniquely to the fire.

"I find that the most beautiful things are those that bear the marks of their creation," she says. "The thumbprint on the base, the slight ripple of the wheel—these are the signatures of a moment in time."

With love & intention,

Elena Vance
